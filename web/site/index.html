About
  • Nothing to install, no sign-up, no cookies. Singleplayer downloads about 90 MB once; your browser keeps it, with your settings and your singleplayer character.
  • Multiplayer: your name plus an optional secret (a tripcode) gives you a tag only you can have, and the server saves your character under it. No password to reset: forget the secret and you start fresh with a new tag.
  • Free software (AGPL v3): github.com/Greytide-inc/ss13.live · privacy note
